================================================================================
SYNTHETIC GLOBAL ENTERPRISE — CORPORATE LEAVE & TIME-OFF POLICY
================================================================================

1. POLICY OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
Synthetic Global Enterprise believes in supporting work-life harmony, physical well-being, and personal recovery for all regular staff and interns. This document codifies eligibility, accrual mechanisms, notice requirements, approval workflows, and encashment terms for all authorized leave categories.

2. AUTHORIZED LEAVE CATEGORIES & ENTITLEMENTS

Category A: Annual Earned Leave (AL)
- Entitlement: 18 working days per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per completed calendar month of active service.
- Advance Notice: For planned absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, employees must submit a formal request via the HRMS portal at least two (2) weeks prior. For single-day planned leaves, a minimum of 48 hours notice is required.
- Approval Authority: Direct Reporting Manager approval is mandatory.
- Carry-Forward & Lapse Rules: A maximum of 10 unused Annual Leave days may be carried over to the following calendar year. All accrued days exceeding 10 lapse automatically on December 31.

Category B: Sick & Medical Leave (SL)
- Entitlement: 10 working days per calendar year, credited upfront on January 1 (or pro-rated upon joining date).
- Notification: Employees must notify their Direct Manager and squad before 09:30 AM on the day of unplanned illness.
- Medical Documentation: A certified Medical Practitioner certificate is required for continuous absences exceeding three (3) consecutive days.
- Encashment: Sick leave cannot be encashed or carried forward. Unused balance lapses annually.

Category C: Casual Leave (CL)
- Entitlement: 6 working days per calendar year for unforeseen personal emergencies or urgent personal business.
- Constraints: Casual Leave cannot be combined with Annual Leave and cannot exceed two (2) consecutive days per instance.

Category D: Parental & Caregiver Leave
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for eligible female employees, extendable by 4 weeks upon medical recommendation.
- Paternity Leave: 15 working days of paid leave to be taken within six (6) months of child arrival.
- Adoption / Primary Caregiver Leave: 12 weeks of paid leave following formal legal adoption.

3. LEAVE APPLICATION & ESCALATION WORKFLOW
- Step 1: Submit digital application through hrms.synthetic-domain.internal/leave.
- Step 2: Reporting Manager reviews and acts within forty-eight (48) business hours.
- Step 3: If no response is received within 48 hours, the request automatically escalates to Department Head for administrative review.
- Emergency Leave: In catastrophic circumstances where portal access is unavailable, direct SMS or telephone contact with the Manager followed by retrospective submission within 24 hours of return is accepted.
