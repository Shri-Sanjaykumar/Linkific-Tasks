\section{1. Architectural Scope \& Prototype Boundaries}
\textbf{FinDoc-AuditEngine v3.0} is a working prototype and production-oriented reference implementation built with FastAPI, Pydantic v2, and scikit-learn.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{In-Memory Audit Ledger (\texttt{AUDIT\_LEDGER}):} Held in a Python list in RAM for fast demonstration and CSV export; records reset when the process restarts.
    \item \textbf{Reproducible Synthetic Baseline (=1{,}000$, \texttt{seed=42}):} The 200-tree \texttt{IsolationForest} is trained on a seeded synthetic baseline to demonstrate anomaly scoring. Production deployment requires calibration on real historical company invoices.
    \item \textbf{Engineered \text{--}100$ Risk Indicator:} Represents an engineered anomaly-risk indicator derived from isolation tree path depth, not a calibrated probability of fraud.
\end{itemize}

\section{2. 4-Stage Pipeline Specification}

\midrule
\textbf{Stage 1: Privacy Gate} & \texttt{app/core/privacy.py}\newline \texttt{FinancialPrivacyScrubber} & 6 compiled regex rules & Masks PAN, Aadhaar format, Bank Account, Card, Phone, Email into \texttt{[TOKEN\_NN]}. \\
\addlinespace[4pt]
\textbf{Stage 2: 3-Way Match} & \texttt{app/services/matcher.py}\newline \texttt{ReconciliationMatcher} & Deterministic SKU rules & Checks $\Delta P_i \le \.01$, {\text{inv},i} \le Q_{\text{rec},i} - Q_{\text{rej},i}$, and $\.05$ subtotal tolerance. \\
\addlinespace[4pt]
\textbf{Stage 3: Anomaly + XAI} & \texttt{anomaly\_detector.py}\newline \texttt{AnomalyScorer} & \texttt{StandardScaler} + 200-tree \texttt{IsolationForest} & Evaluates 7 features + single-feature perturbation $\Delta R_k(\mathbf{x}) = \max(0, R(\mathbf{x}) - R(\mathbf{x}^{(k \leftarrow \mu_k)}))$. \\
\addlinespace[4pt]
\textbf{Stage 4: Tier Router} & \texttt{app/services/router.py}\newline \texttt{ApprovalRouter} & 5 governance queues + RAM ledger & Routes to Tier 1 STP, Tier 2 Manager, Tier 3 Director, Rejected Discrepancy, or Frozen Fraud Risk. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
